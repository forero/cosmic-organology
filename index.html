02

In 1965 two radio astronomers accidentally caught the echo of the early universe. That echo left a mark on how galaxies are arranged today — baryon acoustic oscillations, a sound wave fossilized in space. In 2025 the DESI collaboration used that fossil, measured across fifteen million galaxies, to report the strongest evidence yet that dark energy is changing over cosmic time.

How does a sonic fossil that may be rewriting the fate of the universe feel, in the body?

03

Transduction

Four cosmic epochs mapped by DESI — nearby galaxies, distant galaxies, quasars — become four sculptural organs: instruments that turn imperceptible physical quantities into sound, vibration and light.

Data

Public DESI DR1 catalogs, with DR2 forthcoming. BAO scale and expansion history, computed per tracer and epoch, become the working parameters of the piece.

Critical Organology

A line of practice that reads instruments — biological or technological — as cultural and epistemic mediators of perception, extended here to the acoustic fossil of the cosmos.

05

Artistic Precedents

2022–2023

Fósil Acústico

Santiago Reyes Villaveces & Daniel Villegas Vélez

A sound installation at Baluarte de Santa Catalina, Cartagena de Indias — a sculptural reproduction of the inner ear emitting a continuous drone of synthesized sounds and field recordings from Cartagena, Ciénaga de Juan Polo and La Boquilla, turning the exhibition space into a resonance chamber. Winner, Túnel de Escape 2022, Ministerio de Cultura de Colombia.

2021

Gravitational Organology

Santiago Reyes Villaveces

An instrumental sculpture transducing gravitational waves, proposed for the artist residency of the MIT Kavli Institute for Astrophysics and Space Research — second place in that international competition, and the first articulation of the critical-organology line this project carries into cosmic cartography.

06

Cosmological Context

DESI's dark-energy result is the piece's raw material, and it has had a public life well beyond cosmology journals: first as a startling press moment in 2024, then as a recurring story as the collaboration's evidence hardened in 2025. The coverage below is what put "the universe's fate may be changing" into general circulation — the same claim this project transduces into sculpture.

2024

Dark Energy May Be Weakening, Major Astrophysics Study Finds

Quanta Magazine

One of the first public reports on DESI's year-one result and what an evolving dark energy would mean for the fate of the universe.

2024

DESI's 3D Map of the Universe

TIME, Best Inventions 2024

DESI's survey named one of TIME's Best Inventions of the year, for the largest three-dimensional map of the cosmos ever made.

2025

Is Dark Energy Getting Weaker? New Evidence Strengthens the Case.

Quanta Magazine

A year later, a larger dataset turned the original hint into a stronger case.
